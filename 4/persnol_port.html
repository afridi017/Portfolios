// Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know

What makes 7th Wonder unique?
+

7th Wonder combines premium materials, ethical craftsmanship, and modern design philosophy. Every piece is created with attention to detail, unique aesthetic, and a story to tell. We're not just clothes — we're wearable wonder.

Do you ship internationally?
+

Yes! We ship worldwide. Standard shipping to Pakistan takes 3-5 business days, while international orders arrive within 10-15 business days. Express shipping options are available at checkout.

What's your return policy?
+

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unworn items with tags attached. Simply contact our team, and we'll guide you through the easy return process. Custom or limited edition pieces may have different terms.

How do I find my size?
+

Each product page has a detailed size guide with measurements. If you're unsure, reach out to us on WhatsApp with your measurements and we'll help you find the perfect fit.

Are your materials sustainable?
+

We're committed to sustainability. We source ethical materials, work with certified suppliers, and follow responsible manufacturing practices. Learn more about our sustainability journey on our story page.

Do you offer wholesale or partnerships?
+

Yes! We're always open to meaningful partnerships and wholesale opportunities. Contact us via email at partnerships@7thwonder.com with your inquiry, and our team will get back to you within 48 hours.

When do you release new collections?
+

We release main seasonal collections quarterly, with limited drops and capsule collections in between. Subscribe to our newsletter or follow us on Instagram to be the first to know about new releases.
